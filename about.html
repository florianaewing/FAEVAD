About

Florian Anthony Ewing

Florian Anthony Ewing is a Seattle-based visual artist and data scientist. The pieces in this catalogue were primarily produced at his workshop in View Ridge. His work has been hosted in cafes and other locations around Seattle since 2021.

Prints and framed originals are available directly from the Inks and Watercolors galleries. For current exhibitions, commissions, or anything else, get in touch via the Contact page.

A Note From the Artist on AI Usage

No AI tools were used to produce or alter any of the original artwork hosted here or posted across any of the social media platforms associated with FAEVAD. I wrote any and all of the text that appears in the form of titles, captions, descriptions, and otherwise, the old fashioned way by using my brain, my fingers, and my keyboard.
